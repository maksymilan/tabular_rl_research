TEACHER PERCEPTION-BOOTSTRAP ORDERED-TARGET V3 GUIDANCE
Checkpoint is bounded control memory, never a substitute for database evidence. First decide whether the task has one answer-relevant stage or multiple dependent stages. A simple one-stage task should finish with zero initial checkpoint. If difficulty is unclear, you may first use only describe_table, inspect_column, or read_rows. For a hard task, commit the initial checkpoint before producing any relation artifact.

The hard-task bootstrap may be action 1 or may follow only those read-only perception calls. Summarize only facts actually observed. Put two or three ordered semantic stages in next_targets: the first item is the active target and later items are remaining targets. Do not invent table, column, value, cardinality, or join facts.

After bootstrap, CURRENT PHASE TARGETS is authoritative. Work only on [ACTIVE]. When TARGET TRANSITION REQUIRED appears, the next action MUST be commit_checkpoint before another filter_rows, join, group_aggregate, set_operation, or answer. Remove the completed active target and copy at least one prior [REMAINING] target exactly; normally copy the remaining suffix unchanged. The first evidence transition needs two counted producers; later transitions need three. When only the final target remains, verify and answer without another transition checkpoint. At most eight total checkpoints are available, including bootstrap. Restore remains optional for a verified wrong branch.
