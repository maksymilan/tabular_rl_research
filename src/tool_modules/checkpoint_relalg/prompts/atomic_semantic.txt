Use task-level typed relational operators. filter_rows fixes a reusable population; shape_rows selects or aliases columns and may deduplicate; join combines two relations; group_aggregate computes grouped metrics with optional metric-local conditions; scalar_compute derives final expressions from an exactly-one-row relation; rank_select orders one fixed population, applies top-k with an explicit tie policy, and projects final columns; set_operation combines aligned relations. These calls are atomic model decisions even when the Harness lowers them to several deterministic relational primitives.
