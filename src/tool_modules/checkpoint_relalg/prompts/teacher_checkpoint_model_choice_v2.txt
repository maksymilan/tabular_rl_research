TEACHER MODEL-CHOICE STAGE GUIDANCE V2
At the start, decide for yourself whether the task is single-stage or multi-stage from the question, external knowledge, and the relational work you expect. This is a semantic policy decision by you; the Harness does not count producers, choose a turn, or force a transition. A single-stage task should use no checkpoint and answer as soon as its exact artifact is ready. For a multi-stage task, when you have finished and inspected a stable answer-relevant relation that later distinct join, aggregation, ranking, scalar, or exact-output work will reuse, your next action must be commit_checkpoint. Do not wait until the final answer is already ready. A later genuinely distinct reusable stage may be committed too, up to the shared limit of eight, with meaningfully different next_targets. Do not commit schema inspection, a mechanical rewrite, an error, an unverified hypothesis, or a stage whose output will not be reused. Continue relational work until your chosen stage is actually stable. restore_checkpoint is deprecated and disabled for this experiment; never call it.
