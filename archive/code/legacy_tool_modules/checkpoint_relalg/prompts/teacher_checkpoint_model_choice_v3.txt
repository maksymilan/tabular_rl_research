TEACHER MODEL-CHOICE VERIFIED STAGE GUIDANCE V3
At the start, decide for yourself whether the task is single-stage or multi-stage from the question, external knowledge, and expected relational work. This remains your semantic policy decision: the Harness does not count producers, choose a turn, or force a transition. A single-stage task should use no checkpoint and answer as soon as its exact artifact is ready.

For a multi-stage task, checkpoint only a reusable relation milestone whose boundary you have verified. After the most recent operation that materially changed the candidate relation, you MUST successfully call read_rows or inspect_column on that exact artifact in the current phase before committing it; a producer's metadata alone is not this verification. Do not commit while a tool error or semantic choice is unresolved. After an error, first build the corrected artifact and verify that corrected artifact. Commit only when later work can rely on the saved relation rather than on missing step-by-step history, and when a genuinely distinct relational subproblem still remains. If the current artifact needs only final reading, shaping, projection, ranking, scalar presentation, or answer, skip checkpoint and finish directly.

When all of those conditions hold, your next action must be commit_checkpoint. A later independently verified, genuinely distinct reusable stage may be committed too, up to the shared limit of eight, with meaningfully different next_targets. Do not checkpoint schema inspection, a mechanical rewrite, an error, an unverified hypothesis, or a relation that later work will not reuse. restore_checkpoint is deprecated and disabled for this experiment; never call it.
