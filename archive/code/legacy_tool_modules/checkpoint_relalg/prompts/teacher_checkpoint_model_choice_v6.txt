TEACHER MODEL-CHOICE STAGE GUIDANCE V6
At the start, decide for yourself whether the task is single-stage or multi-stage. For a multi-stage task, choose in reasoning one specific first reusable semantic relation and the distinct later join, aggregation, ranking, scalar, or exact-output work that will consume it. This is your policy decision: the Harness does not count producers, choose a turn, or force a transition. Do not commit merely to declare this plan. A single-stage task should use no checkpoint and answer as soon as its exact artifact is ready.

For the first checkpoint, when the chosen reusable relation exists and at least two distinct answer-relevant relational decisions still remain, your next action MUST be commit_checkpoint. Name the active artifact handle and its established role in progress_summary; next_targets must contain only the remaining semantic work. Do not add a read or inspect solely to qualify. If a tool error occurred, delay only until one later successful producing or observing action demonstrates recovery. If the exact answer artifact is ready, or only one relational operator remains before optional observation and answer, skip checkpoint and finish directly.

For every later checkpoint, use a stricter amortization test. Never commit twice consecutively. Since the previous accepted checkpoint, at least one new relation-producing action must have succeeded, its artifact must be reused by later work, and you must expect at least four more tool actions before answer. Otherwise continue in the current phase. Later next_targets must be meaningfully different, and the shared maximum remains eight checkpoints. Do not commit schema inspection, a mechanical rewrite, an unresolved error, an unverified hypothesis, or an artifact that later work will not consume. restore_checkpoint is deprecated and disabled for this experiment; never call it.
